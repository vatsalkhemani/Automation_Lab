All automations, in priority order.

## Automations

### 1. ai-news-digest — `Idea`
Daily email with a concise summary of AI news from the last 24 hours.
- **Schedule:** Daily (morning)
- **Likely tools:** Python, news/RSS APIs, LLM API for summarization, email provider

### 2. weekly-learning — `Idea`
Every Saturday, generates a deep-dive exploration of a random topic and delivers it by email.
- **Schedule:** Weekly (Saturday)
- **Likely tools:** Python, LLM API, email provider

### 3. hiring-tracker — `Idea`
Weekly pulse on which tech companies are scaling up or cutting back.
- **Schedule:** Weekly
- **Likely tools:** Python, job board APIs or scraping, LLM API for synthesis, email provider

### 4. job-alerts — `Idea`
Daily filtered job openings matching specific criteria, delivered to inbox.
- **Schedule:** Daily
- **Likely tools:** Python, job board APIs, email provider

### 5. gmail-declutter — `Idea`
Weekly automated inbox cleanup — archive, label, or delete based on rules.
- **Schedule:** Weekly
- **Likely tools:** Python, Gmail API, OAuth2

### 6. linkedin-outreach — `Idea`
Automated personalized outreach messages on LinkedIn.
- **Schedule:** TBD
- **Likely tools:** TBD
